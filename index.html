A Brief History of eCash and Cashtab

The concept of eCash dates back to 1983, when cryptographer David Chaum introduced the idea of digital cash that could provide privacy and security for electronic payments. His work became one of the earliest foundations of modern digital currency systems.

In the cryptocurrency era, eCash (XEC) emerged from the Bitcoin ecosystem. On November 15, 2020, the Bitcoin Cash ABC (BCHA) network was created following a split from Bitcoin Cash. Later, on July 1, 2021, Bitcoin Cash ABC officially rebranded to eCash (XEC). The rebrand introduced a new identity, a new ticker symbol (XEC), and the ecash: address format while maintaining the same blockchain.

To support the eCash network, the development team launched Cashtab, the official non-custodial wallet for eCash. Cashtab allows users to store, send, and receive XEC while maintaining full control of their funds.

One of Cashtab's most important features is the ability to import an existing wallet using a seed phrase. A seed phrase is a series of recovery words generated when a wallet is created. By entering the same seed phrase into Cashtab, users can restore access to their wallet, balances, and transaction history on another device. This system ensures that users remain in control of their funds without relying on a centralized service.

Today, eCash continues to develop as a digital payment network focused on fast, secure, and scalable electronic cash for everyday use, while Cashtab serves as one of its primary wallets for managing XEC assets.

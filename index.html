A Brief History of eCash and Cashtab

The concept of eCash dates back to 1983, when cryptographer David Chaum introduced the idea of digital cash that could provide privacy and security for electronic payments. His work became one of the earliest foundations of modern digital currency systems.

In the cryptocurrency era, eCash (XEC) emerged from the Bitcoin ecosystem. On November 15, 2020, the Bitcoin Cash ABC (BCHA) network was created following a split from Bitcoin Cash. Later, on July 1, 2021, it rebranded to eCash (XEC), introducing a new identity and the ecash: address format.

To support the network, the development team launched Cashtab, a non-custodial wallet that allows users to store, send, and receive XEC while maintaining full control of their funds.

Cashtab also supports wallet recovery using a seed phrase, allowing users to restore access on any device securely without relying on centralized services.

Today, eCash continues evolving as a fast and scalable digital payment network, while Cashtab remains one of its key wallet interfaces.

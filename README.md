# history_of_ecash
# The year most commonly cited for the origin of eCash as a concept is 1983.

In 1983, David Chaum published the paper "Blind Signatures for Untraceable Payments," which introduced the cryptographic foundation for anonymous digital cash systems. This work is generally considered the birth of the eCash concept.

A source of confusion is that some of Chaum's related research and development efforts occurred in the early 1980s, and his company DigiCash was founded later in 1989. However:

1983 — First publication of the digital cash concept using blind signatures.
1989 — DigiCash was founded to commercialize the technology.
1990s — DigiCash's eCash system was deployed in pilot programs with banks.

So if you are writing about the first existence of eCash as a digital money concept, 1983 is the correct year to use.
